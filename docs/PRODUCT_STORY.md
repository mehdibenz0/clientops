# Product story

## Why I built this

ClientOps Desk is a fictionalized product mockup inspired by the operating reality of a small family-run B2B service business: many incoming requests are not technically complex, but they still consume time because the team must reconstruct context, search old cases, and decide whether a request is routine or exceptional.

I wanted to build something that felt like a **real internal product**, not a chatbot demo.

## The product thesis

A good operations workspace should:

1. bring client context into the same view as the incoming request;
2. reuse previous resolutions before creating new work;
3. ask for missing information instead of generating low-quality escalations;
4. create a clean internal task when the issue is truly new or sensitive;
5. leave a trace that a human teammate can inspect.

## Why the use case is realistic

The mock business provides corporate lunch wallets and employee meal services to B2B clients. HR and admin contacts send recurring requests about:

- employee eligibility;
- lunch credit activation;
- merchant or partner usage questions;
- order cutoff exceptions;
- invoice discrepancies;
- dietary preference timing;
- refund or balance reconciliation.

Those flows resemble the operational patterns of many B2B service companies while staying distinct from any one real employer's internal tooling.
